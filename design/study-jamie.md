### Jamie

Jamie moved through WeShould pretty quickly and seemed comfortable exploring without much direction. When asked to find something to do with friends, he noticed Paint and Sip as a shared interest with Theo and the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum as an overlap with Nora and Theo. He understood these were shared interests rather than invitations and immediately saw the museum as an opportunity to make plans.

When saving the Leopard Lounge reel, Jamie found "I'd Go" easily but asked, "I'm not actually saying I'm going, right?" He had a similar question after accepting a connection request, wondering whether they could now see what each other wanted to do. Neither question stopped him, but both showed that the consequences of these actions weren't entirely clear.

During the Gardner Museum task, Jamie was happy proposing "Friday evening" and figuring out the exact time later. This was the kind of casual planning I had hoped to support. However, after submitting, he asked, "Wait, is this a plan now, or do they still have to accept?" He understood how to propose an outing but wasn't sure what status it had afterward. When he accepted an invitation in the next task, he recognized why that plan became confirmed.

Jamie also worried that cancelling his Thursday outing might remove the saved activity entirely. Later, after marking the Friday Wilson's Farm outing completed, he initially couldn't find it before locating it in history.

Overall, Jamie could complete the workflow, but sometimes needed reassurance about what happened afterward. Clearer status messages and completion feedback would have helped without changing the casual planning experience that worked well for him.
